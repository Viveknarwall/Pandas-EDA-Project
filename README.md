# Superstore Sales Analysis using Python

## About the Project

This project focuses on exploring and analyzing Superstore sales data using Python. The main goal was to understand sales and profit patterns, compare business performance across different categories and regions, and explore how discounts relate to profitability.

I worked through the dataset using Pandas and created visualizations with Matplotlib and Seaborn to make the results easier to understand.

## Tools & Libraries

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## What I Worked On

- Inspected the dataset and checked its structure, data types, missing values, and duplicate records.
- Reviewed column names and categorical values for consistency.
- Analyzed sales and profit across product categories and regions.
- Explored shipping mode distribution.
- Examined monthly sales trends.
- Investigated the relationship between discounts and profit.
- Created charts and documented the key observations and business recommendations.

## Key Findings

- Technology generated the highest total sales among the three product categories.
- The West region recorded the highest total profit, while Central recorded the lowest.
- Standard Class was the most commonly used shipping mode, accounting for approximately 59.7% of orders.
- The discount vs. profit analysis showed several loss-making orders at higher discount levels.
- Monthly sales fluctuated over time, with some months performing much better than others.

These findings describe patterns in the dataset. Further analysis would be needed to understand the reasons behind them.
